Here is the technical breakdown of how I designed the Multi-Modal Generation Strategy in services/geminiService.ts.
1. The Strategy: "Anchor & Composite"
We use Gemini 2.5 Flash because it is a "Multimodal" model—it can "see" multiple images at once and understand text simultaneously.
My strategy involves two specific steps:
The Anchor (Structure): I pass the user's uploaded room photo as the first reference. I tell Gemini, "This is the geometry. Do not change the walls, floor, or window placement."
The Composites (Products): I pass the actual cropped images of your products (from the database) as secondary references. I tell Gemini, "Here is a sofa. Here is a chair. Place THESE specific objects into the Anchor room."
2. How the Data is Passed (Technical Flow)
The Gemini API accepts an array called parts. We construct this array dynamically:
Part 1: The Prompt (Text) - The set of instructions.
Part 2: The Room (Image) - Encoded as Base64.
Part 3...N: The Products (Images) - Each selected product's image, encoded as Base64.
3. The Exact Prompt Structure
Here is the actual prompt template used in the code. I designed it to force the AI to respect the inputs rather than "hallucinating" random furniture.
code
Text
Professional Interior Design Render. 8k Resolution. Photorealistic.
Task: Redesign the room to look exactly like the user's vision using specific products.

[Style Context]
Style Vibe: Organic Modern. Colors: Cream, Oak, Beige.

ARCHITECTURAL INSTRUCTIONS:
PRESERVE EXACT ARCHITECTURE: [Analysis from Gemini Vision]. 
REFERENCE IMAGE 1: The user's actual room. Keep walls, windows, floor, and perspective EXACTLY as seen in this image. Replace existing furniture.

CRITICAL PRODUCT PLACEMENT INSTRUCTIONS:
You are provided with reference images for specific furniture items. You MUST composite these exact items into the scene.

REFERENCE IMAGE 2: [Seating] Cloud Boucle Sofa.
ACTION: Place this exact item in the room.
Visual Details: Curved cream boucle sofa with low profile and rounded arms.
Dimensions: 84x32x38 inches.

REFERENCE IMAGE 3: [Tables] Travertine Coffee Table.
ACTION: Place this exact item in the room.
Visual Details: Rectangular travertine coffee table with cylindrical legs.

COMPOSITION RULES:
- Harmonious arrangement matching the room's scale.
- Correct lighting and shadows for the inserted products.
4. Why This Works
Visual Fidelity: By sending the actual pixel data of the sofa (Reference Image 2), Gemini replicates the texture (Boucle) and shape (Curved) much better than if we just wrote "Cream Sofa."
Spatial Integrity: By sending the room photo (Reference Image 1), Gemini understands where the floor ends and the wall begins, ensuring the sofa sits on the floor, not floating in space.
