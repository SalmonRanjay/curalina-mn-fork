ALSO DESIGN A BEAUTIFUL HOME PAGE FOR THIS EXPERIENCE

Create a homepage design that sits in front of the 7-step quiz and sells the experience.

Use the SAME design system and tokens defined above:
- Background: #FAF9F7
- Surfaces: white cards with soft shadows
- Brand teal: #24A8AE
- Mint selection: #D9F2E5
- Serif display (Cormorant Garamond) + Inter for body
- 12-column, 1120px max-width centered grid, generous whitespace

HOMEPAGE SECTIONS & CONTENT:

1) Sticky Header
- Left: logo/wordmark.
- Center nav links: “How It Works”, “Style Quiz”, “Pricing”, “The Edit”.
- Right: “Sign In” (text) and a primary button “Start the Quiz”.
- Background: #FAF9F7 with a subtle bottom border. On scroll, add a light shadow.

2) Hero Section (Above the Fold)
- Two-column layout.
- LEFT:
  - Small pill label: “INTERIOR DESIGN, MADE PERSONAL”.
  - H1: “Design the space where you’ll feel most at home”.
  - Subtext: one paragraph explaining the quiz:
    “Discover your signature style in just 7 questions — then get a shoppable design tailored to your room, budget, and lifestyle.”
  - Primary button: “Take the Style Quiz”.
  - Secondary ghost button: “Browse Design Styles”.
- RIGHT:
  - Large hero interior image (beautiful living room).
  - Overlapping floating card at the bottom-left of the image showing a mini mock of the result:
    - Thumbnail render.
    - Text: “Organic Modern Living Room · Curated for you”.
    - Small link: “See an example design”.

3) “How It Works” 3-Step Section
- Background: #FFFFFF.
- Centered section title: “How it works”.
- Three cards horizontally (on desktop):
  1) “Tell us about your space”
     - Icon: simple outline of a room.
     - Copy: “Choose your room, styles and colour palette in just a few clicks.”
  2) “We design it for you”
     - Icon: magic wand / sparkle.
     - Copy: “Our designers curate furniture and decor tailored to your answers.”
  3) “Shop your room”
     - Icon: shopping bag.
     - Copy: “View your rendered room and instantly shop every piece.”
- Add a subtle text link under the cards: “Learn more about our process →”.

4) “Preview the Styles” Carousel
- Background returns to #FAF9F7.
- Title: “Find your look”.
- Horizontal scroll or carousel of style cards:
  - Organic Modern
  - Modern Farmhouse
  - Midcentury Scandi
  - Warm Transitional
  - Contemporary Luxe
  - Artful Eclectic (if included in the quiz)
- Each card:
  - Image thumbnail representing the style.
  - Style name and a short one-liner (e.g., “Soft, calm, natural” for Organic Modern).
  - Hover effect: slight zoom and gradient overlay.
- Clicking a style either:
  - Opens a “Design Styles” page, or
  - Pre-selects that style when the quiz begins (implementation choice).

5) Value Proposition / “Why it’s different”
- Split layout (left text, right visual).
- Left:
  - Title: “Designed by humans, powered by smart tools”.
  - 3 bullet points such as:
    - “Every room is handpicked by professional interior designers.”
    - “Hundreds of premium brands — your design isn’t limited to one store.”
    - “You get shoppable designs — what you see is exactly what you can get.”
- Right:
  - Mock “before/after” card of a room (empty vs fully styled), maybe as a simple static layout or a slider UI concept.

6) Testimonials / Social Proof
- Background: #FFFFFF.
- Title: “Loved by people who care how their home feels”.
- 3 testimonial cards:
  - Quote.
  - Name.
  - Room type (e.g., “1-bedroom condo living room”).
- Keep cards simple: white surface, soft border, small shadow.

7) Bottom CTA Band
- Full-width band with slightly darker neutral: #F3F0EB.
- Centered content:
  - H2: “Your dream room is 7 questions away”.
  - Helper text: “It takes about 3 minutes. There are no wrong answers.”
  - Primary button: “Start the Quiz”.
  - Small text link below: “See an example room first →”.

8) Footer
- Background: #FAF9F7 or #FFFFFF.
- Left: logo + one-line tagline.
- 2–3 link columns:
  - Company: About, Contact, Press, Partner with Us
  - Help: FAQ, Shipping, Returns
  - Resources: Style Quiz, The Edit, Before & After
- Bottom line: copyright, terms, privacy.

INTERACTION & RESPONSIVENESS:
- Make the homepage fully responsive using the same grid system.
- On mobile, collapse the header nav into a hamburger menu.
- Stack hero columns (image below the text).
- Convert 3-column layouts into stacked cards on mobile/tablet.
- Maintain button hierarchy: primary teal CTA stands out clearly, ghost buttons are secondary.

Your output should be:
- Either Figma-ready frame descriptions and/or
- Production-ready front-end layout (e.g., React + Tailwind or HTML/CSS)
that matches this structure and the quiz design system defined above.
